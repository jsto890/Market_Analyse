How this number is made ▾ collapse
Input

4,180 articles in the last 24h from 34 wire and financial sources. Paywalled outlets contribute headline and standfirst only.

Scoring

FinBERT per sentence, averaged per article, then bucketed to a scope by entity match. Neutral sentences are kept, not dropped.

Weighting

Exponential recency decay, 6-hour half-life on the 1-day window. Source reliability multiplier of 0.6–1.0. n is the count after weighting.

What it isn't

Not a price forecast. It measures how the press is writing, which leads sentiment turns but not returns. Below n=40 treat a score as noise.

Overview states a verdict

One sentence, an agreement count, then four cards that each lead with a plain read instead of a status dot. "If you're trading this" turns the regime into named strikes with cost, breakeven and a reason — and says out loud that none survive a break of 636.

Rotation gets its trails

Quadrants labelled on the chart with a line each on what they mean, eight-week trails so direction is visible, and a click-through from a sector to the names you hold in it. An RRG without trails is just a scatter plot.

Macro shows its working

Method published on the page — sources, decay half-life, what n counts, when to ignore a score. Every tile carries a delta and a sparkline, the ±0.05 neutral band is shaded, the benchmark follows the selected window, and every score drills to the articles that made it.
