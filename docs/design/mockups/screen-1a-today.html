Today's tape is new

Everything scheduled to move today on one axis, with a now-marker. Replaces "What to expect", which sits inside a daily brief listing events six days out and duplicates the rail three columns away.

Groups are tabs, top picks are cards

Four stacked tables means the third is never seen. The caveat line prints once instead of four times, and the model's own numbers move to violet so a 0.78 score can't be mistaken for a return.
