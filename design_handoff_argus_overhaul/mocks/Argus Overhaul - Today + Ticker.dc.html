News tone by scope, −1 bearish to +1 bullish. Neutral is anything inside ±0.05.

How this number is made ▾ collapse
Input

4,180 articles in the last 24h from 34 wire and financial sources. Paywalled outlets contribute headline and standfirst only.

Scoring

FinBERT per sentence, averaged per article, then bucketed to a scope by entity match. Neutral sentences are kept, not dropped.

Weighting

Exponential recency decay, 6-hour half-life on the 1-day window. Source reliability multiplier of 0.6–1.0. n is the count after weighting.

What it isn't

Not a price forecast. It measures how the press is writing, which leads sentiment turns but not returns. Below n=40 treat a score as noise.
