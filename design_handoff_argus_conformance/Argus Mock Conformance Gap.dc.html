What is missing is fidelity — the mock's surfaces, sizes, arrangements and a handful of panels that were re-composed from old parts instead of rebuilt.

Sweep text-micro off anything carrying a sentence. Fix the card surface ladder. Add heading to Panel and counts to SegmentedControl, then replace the four hand-rolled switchers. Settle the rail-width question in writing.

Nothing user-visible is added and every page changes — same shape as Phase 1. Every package below assumes it.

P2 The ticker header ~4 days · K-01 to K-08

Rebuild ticker/Header.tsx as the three-band card: fixed zone grid, action row, track-record columns. Price to 28px, add the range and volume bars, lift score + agreement in, retire CatalystStrip, move entry/stop/target onto the chart and retire LevelsCard.

The widest single gap in the app, and the page a user reaches from every other page.

P3 Today, band by band ~4 days · T-01 to T-18

Masthead first (it is the least work and the most visible), then the tape's axis, now-lane and connectors, then the signals band — segmented tabs, toolbar on the tab row, one caveat line above the cards, card hierarchy inverted back. Sector strip to eleven fixed cells last.

Do not add the actual-vs-consensus values here; they arrive with the calendar feed or not at all.

P4 The options group ~5 days · O-01 to O-06

Overview hero + symbol switcher, gamma regime hero + calibrated profile + scenario card, flow's vs-OI and aggressor columns, greeks' mirrored table. Verify the ladder rather than touching it.

Start by confirming /api/odte/unusual returns open interest and side. If it doesn't, O-03 is a backend ticket and the rest of this package proceeds without it.

P5 Rails, Why, and the tail ~3 days · R-01 to R-08, K-09 to K-13, O-07 to O-11

Rail badge colours and quote tracks, news timestamps and the earnings chip, the Why panel's three-leg lead with the ensemble behind a disclosure, macro's methodology unwrapped, portfolio's six-number band.

The Why panel is the one item here that is a design decision rather than a fix — worth agreeing before it is built.

Two things this review cannot settle. The ticker page's right column — the mock shows two cards where the app has seven, and the mock was drawn as the top of the page rather than as a deletion list; someone has to decide what happens to News, Sentiment, History and AI. And the Why panel — the ensemble telemetry it renders is real work and real information, and the mock replaced it with three sentences; the recommendation above keeps both, but that is a product call, not a conformance one.
